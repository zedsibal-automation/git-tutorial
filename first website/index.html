The Stack Behind the Builds

I only write about tools I've used on real projects, including the ones I stopped using.

  • n8n: my default for complex workflows and self-hosting
  • Make: great for fast builds and easy client handoff
  • Zapier: quick wins and simple app connections
  • OpenAI & Claude: classification, drafting, and decision steps
  • Airtable & Google Sheets: logs, data, and simple dashboards
  • Stripe, Slack & Gmail: where the real business actions happen
